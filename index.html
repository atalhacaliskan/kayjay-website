Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with KayJay.

Are you fully insured and bonded?

Yes, KayJay Cleaning Services LLC is fully insured and bonded. We provide Certificates of Insurance (COI) listing your property management or business entity as an additional insured upon request.

Do you offer after-hours and weekend cleaning?

Absolutely. We structure our janitorial schedules around your business hours—offering flexible late evening and weekend shifts—to ensure zero disruption to your daily operations.

How quickly can you provide a proposal for my facility?

After an initial walkthrough or details submission via our contact form, we deliver custom, itemized commercial proposals within 24 hours.
