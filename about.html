Our Story & Mission

Built Around Reliability, Detail & Personal Service

KayJay Cleaning Services LLC was created with a simple goal: provide dependable, detail-focused cleaning with the kind of direct communication that can be hard to find with larger cleaning companies.

As a locally owned and owner-operated business, we take a hands-on approach to every account. We get to know the property, understand what matters most to the client, and build a cleaning plan around the actual needs of the space.

From offices and apartment communities to turnovers and commercial spaces, our focus is simple: show up, communicate clearly, pay attention to the details, and provide service our clients can rely on.

"We want our clients to know exactly who they're working with and feel confident that their property will be cared for. For us, good service comes down to consistency, communication, and doing the little things right."

— KayJay Cleaning Services
