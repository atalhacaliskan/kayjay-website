Our Story & Mission

Built on Real Industry Experience & Strict Standards

KayJay Cleaning Services LLC was established after years of observing a common problem in commercial cleaning: many service providers lack consistency, fail to follow detailed schedules, and don't provide proper quality oversight.

Drawing from extensive background in facility management and hands-on operational experience, our leadership team decided to create a company that property managers and business owners could count on every single day.

We brought together a dedicated, fully vetted team and structured every service around detailed site-specific checklists, reliable communication, and complete insurance coverage.

"We built KayJay on a simple promise: deliver reliable, top-quality cleaning every time without excuses. Every property we care for is treated with maximum attention to detail and respect."

— Founder's Statement
